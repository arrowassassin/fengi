# Fengi mushroom identity

The original relaxed gaming mushroom mark follows the user's chosen teal mascot direction. The raster concepts were inspected as art direction; the shipped SVGs are separately constructed paths, not embedded raster traces. No third-party mascot, stock art or external font is embedded.

`public/brand/` contains full mascot and compact head variants, each in light, dark and one-ink versions. The cap carries a directional pad and two round buttons; the full character holds a generic controller. The compact head omits hands, controller and shoes for small UI uses. The app uses the light/dark head in navigation, the full character on the homepage, and the head as its favicon. All files have viewBoxes, no scripts, no external references and no embedded images.

Keep at least one eye-width of clear space. Use the head at 32px and above; use the full character at 96px and above. For one-ink printing, the white areas are knockouts: confirm garment substrate and separation with the printer. Screenprint, embroidery stitch count, color proofs and physical samples have not been tested. These are usable vector masters, not trademark clearance. A commercial mark search remains required before merchandise investment.
